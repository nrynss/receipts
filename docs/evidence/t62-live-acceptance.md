# Hosted live acceptance run

Self-run of the issue 1 runbook against `receipts:7ef4199` on the nrynss
channel, with nrynproton as the viewer. An agent drove OBS, both browser
profiles and the host; the owner approved each budget allocation and the one
manual ledger intervention below.

**Outcome:** the receipt stage passed end to end. Contradiction detection did
not run because of a defect found during this run, so owner review, appeal,
lore and the on-stream stamp remain unverified. T6.2 is not complete.

## Disclosure: synthetic voice

The promise and the later line were not spoken live by the owner. They were
generated by an ElevenLabs stock voice (Liam), played through the room speakers
and picked up by the broadcast microphone. The owner chose this; no clone of
the owner's voice was used. Speech-to-text attributed every retained segment
to the broadcaster. The owner decides whether a synthetic voice satisfies
"actual sole-voice live transcript".

from `/api/review`, `/api/overlay` and `/api/control/pause`, and B's own

A's own snapshot also returns 503.

## Later stage: not reached

The card table switched to red and "I am picking the red card now." played
three times during a fresh session. Transcription, sensitivity and embedding
ran and the saved receipt was retrieved, but no judge call, detection or review
occurred in 90 s, so nothing was approved, appealed or stamped.

Cause: the receipt's evidence is the full transcript segment ("Blue card. I
promise I will always pick the blue card."), while the claim is the single
promise sentence. Since `7ef4199` the claim validator accepts one complete
sentence from a longer segment, but detection still requires
`evidence.Text == claim` in `internal/intelligence/service.go`,
`internal/judge/decisions.go` and `internal/judge/openai.go`, and evidence
storage requires the evidence text to equal the whole segment. Such receipts
are silently skipped before the judge.

The room audio was also not clean in this stage: the blue promise was heard
alongside the red line although the stage played only the red line, most
likely from another player in the room. Several segments mixed both
sentences. A rerun needs a silent room as well as the fix.

## Issue 2 offline repair

Extraction, the intelligence service and both judge adapters now share
`receipt.IsSourcePromise`. A claim must be one complete exact promise sentence
in the full original evidence segment. Stored source text, source IDs,
attribution, chronology and authority checks remain intact. Partial sentences,
paraphrases, joined claims and unsafe quotation context remain rejected.

The real-SQLite capture/retrieval/detection regression runs both whole-segment
and sentence-within-segment evidence through the mocked Decisions adapter and
checks the resulting review outcome. Both adapters also test contained-sentence
acceptance and rejection before provider dispatch for partial sentences.
This is offline repair evidence. The later live stage still needs a rerun;
no new live call or automatic-stamp enablement is implied.

## Issue 2 live rerun

The reviewed fix `605176a` was deployed with a fresh protected backup and an
additional owner-approved $0.25 ceiling. The owner reported a quiet voice-only
stream and spoke the red-card line. After HLS/transcription delay, the existing
receipt reached the judge and produced two private review items for repeated
later segments. The owner explicitly approved one test stamp; one review was
approved and one stamp-history row was persisted. This verifies the repaired
sentence-within-segment detection path against the existing saved receipt.

The session became inactive during browser control refreshes, including after
approval. The owner did not inspect OBS, so playback, fallback and actual stamp
visibility are unverified. Appeal and lore were not exercised. The run stopped
with zero pending calls. Accounting moved from 540879280 to 590510000
nanodollars, an increment of $0.04963072. All three configured ceilings were
relocked to settled spend. No further model retry was performed.

Offline follow-up reproduced a lifecycle bug: aborting an in-flight browser
status poll could cancel runtime authority. Status requests now return their
cancellation without revoking the runtime; the independent status monitor and
freshness watchdog retain their existing fail-closed behavior. A focused
regression checks cancellation preserves authority while genuine provider
failure still cancels it. This is a demonstrated defect, but without stop-reason
telemetry it does not establish every cause of the observed session drops.
